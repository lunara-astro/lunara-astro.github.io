Lunara Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 10, 2026

Lunara ("the app", "we") is an astrology app published by Betül Kültür. This policy explains what data the app handles, where it goes, and your rights.

1. The short version

  • There is no account and no login. Your people, birth charts and chats are stored on your device.
  • When you use an AI feature, the text needed for that reply (including birth details of the people involved) is sent through our server to OpenAI to generate the answer. It is not stored on our server.
  • We show no ads, do no tracking across other apps or websites, and never sell your data.

2. Data stored on your device

The app keeps the following in a local database (SQLite) and local storage on your phone:

  • your own profile and the people you add: names or nicknames, birth date, birth time and birth place, and relationship labels you choose;
  • generated birth charts and their interpretations;
  • your chat history with the AI, and short notes the AI keeps about what you told it (people and situations you mentioned) so later chats can pick up where you left off;
  • if you ask for a check-in reminder, one scheduled local notification (created on your phone, not sent through any server).
  • app settings (for example language) and a random device identifier.

This data stays on your device. We cannot see it, and it is not backed up by us. Deleting a person removes their data; uninstalling the app deletes everything.

3. Data sent off your device

| What | To whom | Why | |---|---|---| | The messages of the current chat, including birth details and chart data of the people in it | Our AI proxy (Cloudflare Workers), which forwards them to OpenAI | To generate the AI reply | | A random device identifier | Our AI proxy | To apply the free daily message limit | | The same random identifier as your RevenueCat app user ID, plus purchase receipts | RevenueCat and Apple App Store / Google Play | To manage subscriptions and check premium status | | Your IP address (technically required for any connection) | Our AI proxy (Cloudflare) | To apply a per-network daily limit against abuse |

OpenAI. Chat content is sent via the OpenAI API. Under OpenAI's API terms, API data is not used to train their models by default, and it may be kept by OpenAI for a limited time (typically up to 30 days) for abuse monitoring. See openai.com/policies.

Our proxy. The proxy does not store message content. It keeps only daily usage counters keyed by device identifier and by IP address, which expire automatically after 48 hours, and a 10-minute cache of whether an identifier has premium.

RevenueCat / stores. Payments are processed by Apple or Google; we never see your card details. RevenueCat receives purchase information tied to the random identifier.

We do not use analytics or crash-reporting services at this time. If we add them, we will update this policy; they will never receive birth details or chat text.

4. Legal basis (GDPR / KVKK)

  • Performance of a contract (providing the features you ask for): processing chat content and birth details to generate replies; managing subscriptions.
  • Legitimate interest: usage limits and abuse prevention (device and IP counters).
  • Explicit consent: birth details you enter about yourself and others are sent to the AI provider only after you agree in the app. You can stop at any time by not using AI features.

When you add other people, you are responsible for having a good reason to enter their details and for respecting their wishes.

5. International transfers

OpenAI, RevenueCat and Cloudflare may process data outside Turkey and the EU/EEA (for example in the United States). These transfers rely on the providers' standard contractual clauses or other safeguards required by GDPR and KVKK (Law No. 6698).

6. Retention

  • On your device: until you delete it or uninstall the app.
  • Proxy counters: 48 hours. Premium cache: 10 minutes.
  • OpenAI: as described in OpenAI's API data policy (typically up to 30 days).
  • Purchase records: as required by Apple/Google, RevenueCat and tax law.

7. Your rights

Under GDPR and KVKK (Article 11) you may ask to know whether your data is processed, access it, correct it, delete it, restrict or object to processing, receive it in a portable form, withdraw consent, and complain to a supervisory authority (in Turkey, the Personal Data Protection Authority, KVKK; in the EU, your local data protection authority).

Most of your data is only on your device, so you can view, edit and delete it in the app directly. For anything else, email support.lunara.app@gmail.com. Because we have no accounts, we may ask for the identifier shown in the app to find related records.

8. Children

The app is not intended for children. You must be at least 13 years old to use it, or at least 16 in the EU/EEA (or the higher age your country requires). We do not knowingly process children's data; contact us if you think a child has used the app.

9. Security

Data in transit is encrypted (HTTPS). Data on your device is protected by your device's own security. No system is perfectly secure.

10. Changes

We will post changes to this policy on this page and update the effective date. Significant changes will be announced in the app.
